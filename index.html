The Reality on the Ground

A System Under Strain

Three compounding failures that leave millions of South Africans without timely, dignified care.

01

Overwhelmed Public Facilities

Public hospitals and clinics are chronically underfunded — leading to overcrowding, supply shortages, and crumbling infrastructure. Without appointment or queue systems, patients arrive before dawn simply to secure a consultation slot.

T20 South Africa ↗ PLOS ↗
02

Paper-Based, Fragmented Records

Lost clinic folders and misplaced test results are not isolated incidents — they are a systemic failure. Administrative errors delay diagnosis, compromise treatment decisions, and put patients at direct risk of harm.

PubMed Central ↗
03

No Intelligent Triage or Prioritisation

Without smart triage, emergencies queue behind routine consultations. Frustrated patients skip appointments or move between clinics chasing shorter waits — increasing medical errors, eroding trust, and letting critical cases slip through undetected.

PLOS ONE ↗
These are not isolated failures — they are interconnected. Fix one without the others and the system still breaks.

Stateless & Scalable by Design
MedIntel's serverless architecture on Vercel means API endpoints scale horizontally on demand — zero infrastructure management, minimal cost overhead, and instant global delivery. Supabase Row Level Security ensures data is strictly isolated per user role, with no cross-role data leakage. The OCTAVIA IoT simulator runs as a persistent Node.js service on Railway - continuously generating patient vitals every 30 seconds for active patients, hosted independently from the Vercel frontend

Customer Relationships
  • Role-based portals with access-controlled experiences

Customer Segments
